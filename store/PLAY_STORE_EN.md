## Title (≤30 chars)
ScreenMotion Live Wallpaper

## Short description (≤80 chars)
Tilt, touch & play — cinematic interactive live wallpapers with gyro & mascots.

## Full description
ScreenMotion turns your lock and home screens into playful, cinematic worlds.

**What you get**
• **Gyro parallax** — tilt your phone and layers drift with you  
• **Touch & swipe** — drag fish and birds, swipe meteors, boost vehicles  
• **Four themes** — Space, Aquarium, Vehicle, Nature  
• **Vehicle picker** — sports car, truck, motorcycle, helicopter  
• **Expressive mascots** — friendly ship, curious fish, leaf buddy, birds  
• **Light sound effects** — mute anytime in the app  

**How to use**
1. Open ScreenMotion and pick a theme  
2. Play in the live preview (tilt + touch)  
3. Tap **Set as live wallpaper** and confirm  

Designed dark and premium — made for people who love motion, not static images.
